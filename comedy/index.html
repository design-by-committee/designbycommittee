Websites for Edinburgh Fringe shows

If you’re taking a show to the Fringe in August 2027, your posters, flyers and listing all need somewhere to send people. We’ll have your site ready well before the programme launches, so it’s working for you through previews and the whole run.

  1. Winter Get in touch and book a start date.
  2. Spring Your site goes live, in time for press releases and early ticket sales.
  3. June The Fringe programme launches. Your listing, flyers and posters all link to the same page.
  4. August During the run, you add new reviews and star ratings yourself as they come in.

How it works

This is the same process we used for Fallout Theater, Mad Cow Comedy and Comedy on Tap.

  1. 1 Intro call

    A free 20-minute call about your show or venue, your audience and your deadline. Afterwards you get a fixed quote.

  2. 2 Content

    We send a checklist for your bio, photos, videos and ticket links, and help write or edit anything you’re unsure about.

  3. 3 Design and review

    We design and build the site, then send a private preview link. You check it on your phone and laptop and send us one list of changes.

  4. 4 Launch and training

    We launch on your domain and show you how to add shows, change lineups and update reviews, so you don’t rely on us for day-to-day changes.

Questions

If your question isn’t answered here, ask us directly.

Why WordPress?

You own the site, it’s easy to update, and it works with almost every ticketing, mailing list and video tool comedians already use. If you ever want to move on, any WordPress developer can take it over.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Adding a gig, changing a lineup or adding a new review takes a minute or two, and every launch includes a walkthrough so you know where everything is.

Which ticketing platforms do you work with?

Anything that gives you a link or an embed, including the Fringe box office, Eventbrite, Crowdwork, Ticket Tailor, DICE, Confirmed.show and most venue box offices.

I’m only at the Fringe for August. Is a website worth it?

The Show package is designed for a festival run. After August, the same site becomes the home for your tour, a transfer or next year’s show with a few edits.

Do I need my own hosting and domain?

No. We can register your domain and host the site for you on an optional monthly plan, or set everything up on accounts you already have.

Do you work outside the UK?

Yes. We work remotely with comedians, shows and venues across the UK and Europe, and we price and invoice in euros.

Can you work to a tight deadline?

Tell us your date when you get in touch. If the timeline is too tight for the full package, we’ll say so and suggest a smaller first version that can launch sooner.
